# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads define quality strategy and ensure features meet acceptance criteria before release. They coordinate testing efforts, validate quality standards, and identify issues early to reduce production incidents.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and test plans aligned with project phases
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Validate acceptance criteria and quality standards
- Identify and triage bugs, working with developers on resolution
- Report quality metrics and testing progress to the Project Manager
- Collaborate on test automation and infrastructure improvements

### Interactions
- Works closely with Developers on test implementation and bug triage
- Collaborates with Product Managers to understand requirements and acceptance criteria
- Reports quality metrics and testing status to Project Manager
- Partners with Release Manager on smoke testing and pre-deployment validation
- Advises on quality risks and mitigation strategies

### Goals
- Ensure quality standards are met before release
- Catch issues early in the development cycle to reduce production incidents
- Maintain high test coverage and reliable test infrastructure
- Enable fast, confident delivery through effective quality practices

### Typical Communication
- Quality reports and test status in project standups
- Bug tracking and resolution updates
- Test plan reviews with Product and Project Managers
- Pre-release validation sign-off

---

## Tech Lead / Solutions Architect

### Role Summary
Tech Leads define technical approach and architecture, mentor developers, and identify technical risks. They ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with long-term technical strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical approach, architecture, and design decisions
- Conduct architecture reviews and code reviews for critical components
- Mentor developers and foster knowledge sharing
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Advise on technology choices and technical trade-offs
- Support capacity planning and effort estimation

### Interactions
- Partners with Product Managers on feasibility assessment and technical constraints
- Collaborates closely with Developers on implementation approach and design
- Advises Project Manager on technical risks, dependencies, and timeline implications
- Works with other Tech Leads across teams on integration points and shared concerns
- Supports QA in understanding technical testing strategy

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and well-documented technical solutions
- Reduce technical debt and improve system reliability over time
- Enable knowledge sharing and team growth through mentoring
- Align technical decisions with business goals and long-term roadmap

### Typical Communication
- Technical design documents and architecture reviews
- Code review comments and mentoring feedback
- Technical risk assessments in planning and execution
- Cross-team technical alignment discussions

---

## UX/Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX/Design Leads define user experience and design specifications, validate usability, and ensure design consistency. They advocate for user needs and drive intuitive, satisfying user experiences.

### Responsibilities
- Define user experience vision and design specifications for features
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design systems documentation
- Validate design solutions against acceptance criteria and user needs
- Ensure design consistency and adherence to design standards
- Collaborate on design feasibility and technical implementation

### Interactions
- Works closely with Product Managers to understand user needs and business goals
- Collaborates with Developers on design implementation and feasibility
- Presents designs to stakeholders for feedback and approval
- Partners with QA on usability testing and design validation
- Advises Project Manager on design-related dependencies and timeline impacts

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, usable features that delight users
- Maximize customer satisfaction and reduce friction in user workflows
- Align design with product strategy and brand standards
- Build shared understanding of user needs across the team

### Typical Communication
- Design specifications and prototypes in planning phase
- Usability feedback and design validation results
- Design system updates and consistency guidance
- Stakeholder design reviews and feedback sessions

---

## Release Manager / DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
Release Managers and DevOps Engineers manage deployment pipelines, infrastructure, and release processes. They coordinate production deployments, minimize deployment risk, and ensure system reliability and observability.

### Responsibilities
- Design, maintain, and improve deployment pipelines and CI/CD infrastructure
- Coordinate production deployments and manage deployment windows
- Manage infrastructure, environments, and configuration management
- Monitor deployments and execute rollback procedures when necessary
- Establish observability, logging, and alerting for production systems
- Document deployment procedures and runbooks for incident response

### Interactions
- Collaborates with Developers on CI/CD improvements and deployment readiness
- Works with QA/Testing Lead on smoke testing and post-deployment validation
- Reports deployment status and production health to Project Manager
- Coordinates with on-call and incident response teams for issue escalation
- Partners with Tech Lead on infrastructure architecture and reliability improvements

### Goals
- Reduce deployment risk and enable fast, confident releases
- Minimize time-to-production and deployment cycle time
- Ensure system reliability, uptime, and observability in production
- Enable rapid incident response and recovery

### Typical Communication
- Deployment status updates and release notes
- Infrastructure and pipeline improvements in planning
- Post-deployment validation and sign-off
- Incident reports and reliability metrics

---

## Sponsor / Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors provide business context, strategic alignment, and executive decision-making for projects. They remove organizational blockers, approve major decisions, and ensure projects deliver business value aligned with organizational strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Provide business context and strategic alignment for the project
- Review and approve major project decisions and scope changes
- Identify and remove organizational blockers and resource constraints
- Monitor business outcomes and project ROI
- Advocate for the project with senior leadership
- Ensure project aligns with organizational priorities and goals

### Interactions
- Receives regular status updates and milestone reports from Project Manager
- Makes go/no-go decisions and approves scope/timeline changes
- Escalates issues or removes blockers that Project Manager surface
- Engages with stakeholders and leadership to maintain support and alignment
- Reviews deliverables and business outcomes at key milestones

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers measurable business value
- Align project execution with organizational strategy and priorities
- Mitigate business and organizational risks
- Enable team success by removing barriers and providing support

### Typical Communication
- Monthly or milestone-based status reports
- Executive decision reviews on major scope or timeline changes
- Business outcome and ROI tracking
- Stakeholder alignment and advocacy communications
